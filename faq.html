🐱 Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I feed my cat?

Adult cats should usually be fed twice a day with nutritious cat food and fresh water available at all times.

2. How often should I bathe my cat?

Most cats only need a bath every 4–6 weeks unless recommended otherwise by a veterinarian.

3. Why is my cat not eating?

If your cat refuses food for more than 24 hours, contact a veterinarian as soon as possible.

4. How can I keep my cat healthy?

Provide healthy food, clean water, regular exercise, vaccinations, and routine veterinary checkups.

5. How often should I clean the litter box?

Clean the litter box every day to keep your cat comfortable and prevent unpleasant odors.
