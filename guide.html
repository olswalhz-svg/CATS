🐱 Cat Care Guide

🍽 Feeding Your Cat

Feed your cat high-quality cat food twice a day and always provide clean, fresh water.

🧼 Grooming

Brush your cat's fur regularly to remove loose hair and prevent tangles. Trim its nails when needed.

🏥 Health Care

Visit the veterinarian regularly for vaccinations, checkups, and treatment if your cat becomes sick.

🏃 Exercise

Keep your cat active by providing toys, scratching posts, and time for play every day.

🧹 Litter Box

Scoop the litter box daily and replace the litter regularly to keep it clean and odor-free.
